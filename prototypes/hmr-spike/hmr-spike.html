Edit a running app without losing what you typed

The question: gable's hot-reload plan says that when you edit a component or service, the framework builds a new copy, carries the old copy's data across by field name and kind, throws the old copy away, and rebuilds the view. A service edit also refreshes everything that uses that service. All edits in one save are applied together or not at all.
